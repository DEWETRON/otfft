Same library source, one thread, N = 2^18, library fwd0 (median of 41), measured on the Ryzen 9 3900X.
Different small test programs that include src/otfft_algo_sixstep.cpp give different times:
  phases3  1,440 to 1,490 us   (three runs)
  phases4  1,980 to 2,060 us
  fx2      2,590 to 2,640 us
Disabling address space randomisation does not change it (phases4: 1,919 to 1,989 us in 4 runs, 1,931 to 1,976 us with
randomisation in 8 runs). Data pattern (smooth, periodic, random) does not change it (phases3: 1,385 to 1,405 us).

  --param max-inline-insns-single=2000 ... (more inlining)  2,447 us
